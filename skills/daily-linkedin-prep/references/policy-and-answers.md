# Candidate policy and answers

## Source precedence

Use later explicit user corrections first, the approved application profile for personal answers, and the detailed verified career inventory for career claims. When the master resume is broader than the inventory, use the narrower supported claim. “Needs detail” is not verified evidence. Do not turn internal testing into a claim of external production users, or prototype/transition work into ownership of production scaling. Preserve employer-specific scope; an answer about one employer does not automatically establish the same facts for every employer.

The preserved profile is copied verbatim for provenance, including historical workflow directions. The following rules resolve its conflicting instructions for this adaptation:

- Do not automatically attest that all basic qualifications are met merely because the historical profile says to always answer Yes. Answer the actual question from evidence; leave an unresolved attestation for Kayla when the evidence and requested declaration conflict.
- Do not answer every AI-use question No. Distinguish who the applicant is, who submits, and whether AI assisted preparation. Kayla is the applicant and submits herself; this workflow uses AI to prepare materials. Answer the exact question truthfully or leave it for review if its wording cannot be resolved.
- Never claim Kayla has read or accepted a policy without specific approval for that statement. Do not accept unfamiliar terms as a generic checkbox-filling step. Existing narrowly approved communications consent remains usable within its scope.
- Notion tracking follows SKILL.md and the later October 1 manual-upload exception: log verified done applications and parent-verified filled drafts whose QA résumé needs manual upload, even in partial cycles. Other unfinished jobs remain local. Preserve older rows. Use Prepared for Review only for strict done drafts and Preparation Blocked with Needs résumé upload for the exception, always with blank Application Date until independently confirmed human submission. This workflow never submits applications.

For salary and experience filters, distinguish required minima from preferred experience and unrelated experience ranges. Record the exact wording. If a required minimum is three years or more, skip. If there are alternative qualification paths, assess the applicable supported path rather than treating every number on the page as a universal requirement. Use an advertised annual salary range midpoint; if units, currency, compensation basis, or range interpretation are unclear, investigate and record uncertainty. Do not invent salary, GPA, dates, experience duration, clearances, or willingness to relocate beyond the approved New York scope.

## Field plans

Record question, field identity, proposed value, exact source quotation(s), interpretation, and verification result. A quotation must support the answer, not merely occur somewhere in the source file. Do not treat browser autofill or a value from a previous application as evidence. Unsupported factual inferences remain unanswered even if the field is required.

Use exact approved contact facts from the private application profile. Do not infer location equivalence; select only an explicitly approved accurate offered option and verify it persists. Residence, desired work location, current local residency, and future relocation are different questions. A plan to relocate does not establish current residency.

Use the approved first-person motivation baseline only for a direct motivation/fit question. Tailor it to the job with supported relevant evidence; generic optional Additional Information remains blank. Desired salary remains blank when optional and unresolved for Kayla when required unless she provides a value.

Use only explicitly approved demographic answers for the actual voluntary questions, without inferring additional categories or identities. Preserve unknowns. Avoid repeating sensitive personal details in progress messages or the daily summary; keep evidence in private local files and transmit only facts needed for the authorized form.


## Private user corrections

Keep confirmed personal answers, employer-specific exceptions, availability, experience durations, travel preferences, and sensitive demographic choices in the local ignored `user-answers.md`. Apply only explicit approvals with their provenance and scope. The public repository deliberately contains no saved personal answers. Read this local file together with the approved candidate sources before live preparation.
